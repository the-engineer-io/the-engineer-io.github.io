Inkprint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Inkprint is a puzzle game made by Salim Ben Hadj Hassin ("we"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play Inkprint on Android or iOS.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There is no account and no sign-in. Your game progress (solved levels, gold seals and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Anonymous play statistics. To see which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as "level 12 started", "level 12 solved in 95 seconds with 1 hint" or "a hint was used". These events contain no name, no account and no advertising ID; Google may also process your device type, app version and approximate location (country/region from your IP address). Statistics are never used for advertising. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, full statistics are only collected if you agree in the consent form; otherwise Google receives only anonymous signals without any identifier.

2. Advertising

The free version of Inkprint shows ads between levels, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ads choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS, you can control ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

3. Children

Inkprint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

4. Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the game (or using Settings → Reset all progress) deletes it. For data held by Google for advertising and statistics, see the choices in section 2 (statistics follow the same consent choice).

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email io.inkprint.game@gmail.com.
